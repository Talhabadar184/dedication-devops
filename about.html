What is TaskFlow?

TaskFlow is a lightweight, intuitive task-management web application designed to help individuals and small teams keep track of their daily assignments, priorities, and deadlines.

Usage Guide

Follow these simple steps to get the most out of TaskFlow:

1. Dashboard (Home)

Start at the Home page to get a quick overview of your task statistics, including pending and completed task counts.

2. Adding a Task

Navigate to the "Add Task" page to create a new task. You can specify the title, a detailed description, priority (High/Medium/Low), and a due date.

3. Viewing the Task List

Go to the "Task List" to see all your tasks in a table format. Use the filters to quickly show All, Pending, or Completed tasks.

4. Managing Task Details

Click on any task from the list to view its full details. From the Task Detail page, you can mark the task as complete or delete it entirely.
